THE PERFECT FIT STARTS WITH YOU

How to Measure

A few simple measurements help you find your most comfortable fit.

01

Bust

Measure around the fullest part of your bust, keeping the measuring tape level.

02

Waist

Measure around your natural waist without pulling the measuring tape too tightly.

03

Hips

Measure around the fullest part of your hips, keeping the tape straight and level.

04

Inseam

Measure from the top of your inner thigh down to your preferred trouser hem.

A Little Tip

Keep your measuring tape comfortably snug, not tight. Stand naturally and ask someone to help if needed.

Need More Help? →
How do I choose the right size?

Measure your bust, waist, and hips, then compare your measurements with our size chart. If you are shopping for a particular style, check its fit description before choosing your size.

What if I fall between two sizes?

Consider your preferred fit and the fabric. Choose the larger size for a more relaxed feel, or check the product description for guidance on stretch and fit.

Are sizes consistent across all styles?

Fit can vary depending on the cut, fabric, and design. Always refer to the size chart and fit notes provided on the individual product page.

How should I measure myself accurately?

Use a flexible measuring tape and keep it level without pulling it too tight. Measure over lightweight clothing and follow the instructions in our How to Measure section.

Which fabrics offer the most stretch?

Fabrics containing elastane or spandex generally provide stretch. Check each product's fabric composition and description to understand its flexibility and feel.

Can I exchange an item if the fit isn't right?

Please review our return and exchange policy for eligibility, time limits, and instructions. Contact our team if you need help with an order.
